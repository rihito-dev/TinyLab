# Research notes

Keep exploratory reading and open questions here. A note is not a completed experiment or an accepted decision.

Create `YYYY-MM-DD-short-topic.md` when needed, with:

- Question and relevance to the current goal.
- Source IDs from [SOURCES.md](../provenance/SOURCES.md), with specific page or section references.
- Observed facts, interpretations, and unknowns in separate paragraphs.
- Alternatives considered and unresolved contradictions.
- Next action: investigate, create an experiment, make a decision, or stop.

Prefer a short synthesis with source links over copying entire documents. Promote a testable question into `../experiments/` once its conditions can be stated.
