# Conditions

Fill this in and commit before running the experiment. Use `not applicable` with a reason when a field does not apply.

- **Population and scope:** What is included? Over which period?
- **Inputs:** Source IDs, versions, acquisition dates, and immutable locations or hashes where possible.
- **Exclusions:** What is excluded, and how will missing data be handled?
- **Method:** Exact steps, comparison or baseline, parameters, and units.
- **Measurement:** Metric, denominator, sample size, and uncertainty reporting.
- **Acceptance rule:** A threshold fixed before seeing results.
- **Stop rule:** When to stop for failure, lack of evidence, or cost.
- **Budget:** Time, money, API calls, or other constraints.
- **Reproduction:** Commands or manual steps; required environment and dependencies.

After observing results, preserve this plan. Record deviations in [result.md](result.md); create a new experiment for revised conditions.
