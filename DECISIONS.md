# Decisions

Append one entry per meaningful decision. Link evidence; preserve rejected alternatives. When a decision changes, add a new entry that supersedes the old one. Do not rewrite the old rationale to match later results.

The entry below is a blank template, not an accepted project decision. Start real entries at D001.

## DXXX — Short decision title

- **Status:** proposed / accepted / rejected / superseded.
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD.
- **Context:** What decision is needed, and what constraints apply?
- **Decision:** What will you do?
- **Evidence:** Links to experiments and source records; separate facts from assumptions.
- **Rejected alternatives:** What else was considered, and why was it rejected?
- **Cost and consequences:** Time, money, maintenance, and tradeoffs.
- **Revisit when:** A concrete trigger, date, or missing piece of evidence.
- **Supersedes / superseded by:** Decision ID, if applicable.
